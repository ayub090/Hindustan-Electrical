HINDUSTAN ELECTRICAL - SHOP APP
================================

This folder is the whole app. It works like an Android app: it has its own icon,
opens full-screen, and keeps working with NO internet.

To put it on your shop phones you do two things:
  PART 1: put this folder on a free website (one time, on a computer or phone browser)
  PART 2: open that website on each shop phone and tap "Install"


PART 1 - PUT THE FOLDER ON A FREE WEBSITE (one time)
-----------------------------------------------------
Android only lets a web app install from a secure web address (https://...),
so the folder must be uploaded once. Any one of these free hosts works:

  Netlify       - make a free account, use "Add new site" > "Deploy manually",
                  and drag this whole folder onto the page.
  Cloudflare Pages - free account > Workers & Pages > Create > Pages >
                  "Upload assets", then upload this folder.
  GitHub Pages  - free account, make a new repository, upload every file in this
                  folder, then Settings > Pages > deploy from the main branch.

(Host screens change from time to time. If a button has moved, search for
"<host name> upload a static site".)

When it finishes, the host gives you an address like https://something.netlify.app
Write that address down. This is your app's address.

IMPORTANT: always use the SAME address. Your shop data is saved against the address,
so a different address opens an empty copy.


PART 2 - INSTALL ON EACH SHOP PHONE
------------------------------------
1. Open Chrome on the phone and go to your app's address.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap "Install app" (some phones say "Add to Home screen").
4. Tap Install. The lightning-bolt icon appears with your other apps.
5. Open it from the icon. After the first open it works without internet.


THINGS TO KNOW
--------------
* Each phone keeps its OWN stock, sales and bills. Phones do not sync with each other.
  Use ONE phone as the main billing phone.
* BACK UP: at the bottom of the app, tap "Back up data" often. It saves a file you
  can keep in WhatsApp, Google Drive or email. "Restore backup" loads it again, on
  the same phone or a new one.
* Clearing Chrome's data, or uninstalling the app, ERASES the shop data. Back up first.
* First use: the app starts with example products, sales and purchases. Tap
  "Clear all data" at the bottom (it asks twice) and then add your real products
  on the Inventory tab. Add your address, phone and GSTIN under "Shop details" so
  they print on every bill.
* Bills: after saving a bill you can print it (the Print button opens the phone's
  print window, where you can also choose Save as PDF), download it as a page, or
  tap "Copy for WhatsApp" and paste it to the customer.
* Updating the app later: replace the files on your website with new ones. Phones
  pick up the new version the next time they open the app with internet on.
* Do not rename or edit the files inside this folder.

This is not on the Google Play Store. It does not need a Play Store account.
